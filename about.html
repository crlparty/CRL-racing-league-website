About CRL

Championship Racing League

CRL is a community-based esports racing league built around Cup, O’Reilly and Truck competition, with its own rules, procedures, points, records, administration and championship history.

The goal is serious, fair racing while preserving the drivers, rivalries, friendships and memorable races that make CRL what it is.
